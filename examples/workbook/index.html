RayManworkbook: one lens

Click a diagram to work on it in RayMan. When you are done, Back to workbook brings you here, with your work in the diagram. How workbooks work

1. Three rays

The object stands 1.75 focal lengths in front of the lens L1 (f = 50 mm), and the hints are on. From the tip of the object, trace the three rays: the one through the centre of the lens, the one parallel to the axis, and the one through the front focal point. Where do they meet, and how big is the image?

2. Image at infinity

Now the object is at the front focal plane of L1. Trace the three rays from its tip again: where is the image? Then add a second lens, also f = 50 mm, a reasonable distance (say 100 mm) after L1, and trace the rays on through it. Where is the image now, and how big is it?
